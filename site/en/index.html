Beautiful, accurate, free mind maps

XMap Studio is a free and open source alternative to XMind. It opens and saves .xmind files, writes native LaTeX equations and runs on Linux, Windows and in the browser. No account, no telemetry.

Try it online Download for desktop

MIT license · Your files stay on your device

Everything a well-crafted map needs

You work right on the map: type in the topic, add branches from the keyboard, drag to rearrange. How it looks matters as much as what it says.

Native LaTeX equations

Turn a topic into an equation rendered with MathJax. Opened in XMind, it stays visible and editable.

Works with XMind

Uses the .xmind format: XMind maps open here, and maps made here open in XMind.

Edit on the map

Tab and Enter for new topics, drag and drop, relationships, summaries, notes, labels, links and images.

Themes and styles

Colors, shapes, lines and typography of each sheet come from a theme editor, in light and dark.

Sheets, print and PDF

Several sheets in one map. Print on A4 or A3 with page breaks between branches, or export one PDF page as big as the map.

Never lose work

Every save is checked before it touches the disk and leaves a backup. If XMind has the file open, saving stops.

Frequently asked questions

Is XMap Studio free?

Yes. It's free software under the MIT license: free forever, no Pro tier, no ads.

Can I open my XMind files?

Yes, modern .xmind files (XMind Zen and later) open and save. The old XMind 8 format isn't supported.

Are my maps uploaded to a server?

No. Even the web version works entirely in the browser: your files stay on your device. No account, no telemetry.

How do I write a math equation?

Select a topic and pick the equation from the toolbar: type LaTeX and see the MathJax preview.
